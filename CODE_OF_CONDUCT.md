# Community code of conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces should be constructive, technically rigorous, and welcoming. Treat contributors with respect, critique ideas and evidence rather than people, state uncertainty honestly, preserve source and license boundaries, and avoid overstating scientific or operational readiness.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and knowingly false attribution are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, project events, and public representation of the project. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or, when confidentiality is important, through the private security-advisory channel described in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit or remove content, pause participation, or restrict access when needed to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where possible, and avoid retaliation against good-faith reporters. This document governs community behavior; it is not a technical assurance or safety certification.
